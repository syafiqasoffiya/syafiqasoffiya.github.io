Biography

About Me

I am an Information Science PhD student at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM) with an academic foundation in Information Management and Information Systems Management. My academic path has been shaped by a sustained interest in how information is structured, governed, preserved, and shared—both within formal organisations and across communities.

Alongside my postgraduate research, I bring three years of university teaching experience as a Part-Time Full-Time (PTFT) Lecturer at UiTM Kelantan, where I delivered technical and foundational courses spanning programming, database management, computer system operations, electronic publishing, and archival foundations. Before transitioning into academia, I spent four years in IT industry roles with Accenture Sdn. Bhd. and Schlumberger Sdn. Bhd., working in technical support, incident management, enterprise systems, and Office 365 workflow automation.

Combining academic inquiry with practical industry experience allows me to connect theoretical principles in Information Science with real-world workplace applications—whether through lectures, student system development supervision, or my doctoral research on Indigenous Forest Knowledge stewardship and intergenerational knowledge transmission.

Academic Inquiry & Scholarship

Research & Publications

Current PhD Research · In Progress Information Science

“Indigenous Forest Knowledge Stewardship Practices Supporting Intergenerational Transmission Among Selected Orang Asli Communities in Pahang: The Role of Community Authority.”

This doctoral research explores how Indigenous Forest Knowledge (IFK) is practised, stewarded, transmitted across generations, and governed through community authority within selected Orang Asli communities in Pahang, Malaysia. Grounded in Information Science, the qualitative inquiry examines the social and governance mechanisms that sustain community-held knowledge over time.
